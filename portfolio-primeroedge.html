PrimeroEdge is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed exclusively to revolutionize K-12 school nutrition programs and state education agency operations across the United States.

An exceptional UI Developer who brought our school nutrition application to life. They successfully built complex data tables and responsive layouts for our POS and inventory modules, delivering clean code and an outstanding user experience ahead of schedule.

The system bridges the gap between high-speed front-of-house operations and complex back-of-house logistics. It features an intuitive, offline-resilient Point of Sale (POS) application for rapid student meal lines alongside automated student eligibility engines that sync directly with state databases.

By implementing PrimeroEdge, educational institutions can replace outdated, manual workflows with an automated, data-driven framework that maximizes operational efficiency and mitigates financial risk.
